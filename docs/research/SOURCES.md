# Sources

Every document fetched and read for this knowledge base, with what the platform takes from it. Citations elsewhere in `docs/research/` use the short keys below.

## Primary documents read in full or in substantial part

### [CRV-MANUAL] Coordinate Remote Viewing Manual (1986)

- URL read: https://rexresearch.com/articles2/crvmanual.pdf (full transcription, 98 pages)
- Alternate URL: http://www.crvreg.org/remoteviewing/assets/CRVManual.pdf
- Provenance: written at SRI International in 1986 under Harold E. Puthoff's program, drafted by Paul H. Smith from Ingo Swann's training lectures, used as the DIA SUN STREAK training reference.
- Taken from it: the entire CRV protocol. Theory (signal line, matrix, aperture, objectification), the six-stage structure, ideogram/A/B sequencing in Stage I, sensory ordering in Stage II, dimensionals and aesthetic impact in Stage III, the Stage IV matrix columns (S2, D, AI, EI, T, I, AOL, AOL/S), Stage V interrogation of cognitrons, Stage VI three-dimensional modeling, every break type (AOL break, confusion break, too-much break, AI break, EI break, bio break), monitor conduct, personal inclemencies, and session admin formats. This document is the normative spec for the platform's CRV state machine and transcript event types.

### [PUTHOFF-CIA] Puthoff, H. E., "CIA-Initiated Remote Viewing At Stanford Research Institute"

- URL read: https://www.newdualism.org/papers/H.Puthoff/CIA-Initiated%20Remote%20Viewing%20At%20Stanford%20Research%20Institute.htm
- Published in Journal of Scientific Exploration, Vol. 10, No. 1 (1996).
- Taken from it: program origin (1972 Ingo Swann magnetometer demonstration), CIA sponsorship, SCANATE coordinate tasking, Pat Price's West Virginia Site session, the Semipalatinsk URDF-3 gantry crane session, Hella Hammid's nine-target series, the shift from outbound-beacon to coordinate targeting, and the double-blind protocol description that grounds the platform's blindness invariant.

### [PAT-REPORT] Puthoff, H. E. and Targ, R., "Perceptual Augmentation Techniques," SRI Final Report, Project 3183 (Jan 1974 - Feb 1975)

- CIA reference: CIA-RDP96-00787R000100110001-0
- URL read (mirror): https://avalonlibrary.net/Perceptual_Augmentation_Techniques_SRI_1973-1975_(Remote_Viewing_Puthoff_&_Targ)/CIA-RDP96-00787R000100110001-0_(Final_Report_Puthoff_Targ).pdf
- Taken from it: the executive summary of the founding applied-research contract. Long-distance coordinate viewing (West Virginia Site, Semipalatinsk), the technology series with multiple independent viewers per target, the four-state random number generator series, and the methodological defenses that matter for platform design. Every experiment is logged before it is judged, no post-hoc selection, all data unedited in the judged package, and double-blind protocol so no person in contact with the viewer knows the target. These become audit-trail requirements.

### [TART-TTI] Tart, C. T., "Improving Real-Time ESP by Suppressing the Future: Trans-Temporal Inhibition" (Mind at Large chapter)

- URL read: https://www.aapsglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Tart-Improving-Real-Time-ESP-by-Suppressing-the-Future-Trans-Temporal-Inhibition-Mind-At-Large-chapter.pdf
- Taken from it: the ten-choice trainer design with immediate trial-by-trial feedback, the displacement analysis (real-time hits scored at lag 0, and hits against the immediately preceding and following targets at lags -1, +1, and beyond), the finding that talented percipients score above chance in real time while scoring significantly below chance on +1 and -1 displacement, and the trans-temporal inhibition model (the mind sharpens the real-time signal by suppressing temporally adjacent targets, analogous to lateral inhibition in sensory neurophysiology). This is the schema requirement for sequential series, feedback latency, and displacement scoring. See `TART-TTI.md`.

### [CIA-BRIEF] CIA Star Gate program briefing (CRV/ERV/WRV)

- CIA reference: CIA-RDP96-00789R003300210001-2
- URL read: https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/cia/CIA-RDP96-00789R003300210001-2.pdf
- Taken from it: DIA operational definitions of psychoenergetics and remote viewing, the three sanctioned methodologies (Extended Remote Viewing, Coordinate Remote Viewing, Written Remote Viewing), tasking categories and target types, the operational tasking sheet fields (project number, session number, date, method, source identifier, encrypted coordinates), and the report and evaluation formats with accuracy and value ratings. These ground the platform's tasking model and cue types.

### [MAY-FOM] May, E. C., fuzzy-set figure of merit for free-response anomalous cognition

- Primary papers: May, Utts, Humphrey, and colleagues on fuzzy-set analysis of free-response AC (SRI/SAIC technical reports in the Star Gate collection). Summary treatment appears throughout the SAIC era work Utts assessed [UTTS-1995].
- Taken from it: Accuracy is the fraction of the response that describes the target. Reliability is the fraction of the target described by the response. Figure of merit is the product. The platform stores that composite on every judgment. Without a fuzzy-set encoding of imagery, accuracy is the graded rank `(N - rank + 1) / N` and reliability is the process-variable ratio of signal events to signal-plus-declared-AOL. The product stays on the same 0 to 1 scale May used.

### [UTTS-1995] Utts, J., "An Assessment of the Evidence for Psychic Functioning" (1995, for the AIR review)

- URL read: https://www.scientificexploration.org/docs/10/jse_10_1_utts.pdf (JSE Vol. 10, No. 1 reprint)
- Taken from it: the statistical framing the platform must support. Rank-order judging against a pool (response ranked against the true target plus decoys, first-place match probability 1/N under the null), effect sizes comparable across SRI and SAIC laboratories, hit rates near 34 percent where 25 percent is chance, and the recommendation that future work instrument process variables rather than re-prove existence. Grounds the judging tables and population statistics.

### [AIR-1995] American Institutes for Research, "An Evaluation of Remote Viewing: Research and Applications" (Mumford, Rose, Goslin, 1995), via The Classified Record transcription

- URL read: https://theclassifiedrecord.com/documents/stargate/primary-documents
- Taken from it: the closeout context. Utts concluded an anomalous effect is statistically established, Hyman agreed the results are not explained by chance or identifiable flaws but disputed the psychic interpretation, and the operational evaluation found intelligence product too vague and never used standalone, leading to the 1995 termination recommendation. The platform's answer to this history is rigorous per-session instrumentation, which both reviewers said was missing at scale.
